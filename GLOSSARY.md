# Forma

A design is an empty canvas of parts. A cabinet is one kind of group on that canvas.

## Language

**Part**:
One panel, front, or piece of hardware in a design.
_Avoid_: piece, item, component

**Group**:
A named set of parts that are selected together.
_Avoid_: assembly, selection

**Cabinet**:
A group of panels that has a cabinet configuration.
_Avoid_: carcass, unit

**Cabinet configuration**:
The width, height, depth, shelf positions, and interior-panel positions of a cabinet.
_Avoid_: preset, cabinet settings

**Drawer**:
A group of four panels that has a drawer configuration. The decorative front is a separate part.
_Avoid_: drawer box
